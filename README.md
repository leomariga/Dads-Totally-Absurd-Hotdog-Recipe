# Dads-Totally-Absurd-Hotdog-Recipe
Ridiculously good hotdog recipe from my dad

# Ingredients
- One big onion
- One galic clove
- Tomato souce (Quero) - 350g
- Sausages - 500g

# Preparation
- Chop the onion and the garlic in small pieces
- Cut the sausages longitudinally
- Cook the onion with the garlic in a pan until it gets softer and smaller
- Add the tomato sauce and the sausages and cook for around 20 or 30 minutes

# Eating suggestions
- Eat with French bread
- Add shoestring potatoes
- Eat with corn, peas and Parmesan cheese
